About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight application that helps users quickly capture and organize notes.

It is designed to make note-taking simple, fast, and accessible from anywhere.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the QuickNotes application.
  2. Create a new note.
  3. Enter your title and content.
  4. Save the note.
  5. Edit or delete notes whenever needed.
